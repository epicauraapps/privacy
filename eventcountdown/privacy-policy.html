Privacy Policy

Event Countdown · EpicAura Apps

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Event Countdown (package com.epicaura.eventcountdown), made by EpicAura Apps ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no account, no internet access, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, receive or sell any of your data.

1. Information that stays on your phone

The app saves only what you type or choose, in its private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else:

WhatExamples
EventsTitle, description, date and time, end date, repeat setting.
Event styleCategory, icon or emoji, colour.
RemindersWhich reminders you picked for each event.
Background photosA private copy of the photo you choose for an event.
SettingsTheme, countdown style, notification sound, default reminders, recent searches.

2. Information we do not collect

The app has no sign-in and does not use the internet, so nothing leaves your phone through us:

  • No name, email address or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising, advertising ID or tracking.
  • No analytics or crash reports sent to us or to third parties.

3. Photos

  • Photos are chosen with Android's built-in photo picker, so the app never gets access to your whole gallery.
  • The photo you pick is resized and copied into the app's private storage, where other apps cannot reach it.
  • The copy is deleted when you change or remove the photo, or delete the event forever. Your original photo in the gallery is never changed or deleted.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show a reminder when an event is coming up. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
  • Alarms & reminders: only if you turn on "Exact-time reminders" in Settings, so reminders ring at the exact minute.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts, because Android clears them on reboot.

The app does not request internet access.

5. Backups

If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include the app's data (events, settings and photos) in your own Google account backup, so it comes back on a new phone. This is Android's standard backup run by Google; we never receive or read it. You control it in Settings → Google → Backup.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete an event: it moves to Recently Deleted, where you can restore it for 30 days. After 30 days it is removed automatically.
  • Delete forever: "Delete forever" or "Empty all" in Recently Deleted removes events and their photos immediately.
  • Remove everything: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Settings → Apps → Event Countdown, deletes all events, photos and settings from your phone.

7. Third-party content

The animated emoji are Google's Noto Emoji, built into the app and used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Using them sends nothing to Google or anyone else. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code.

8. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox: only Event Countdown can read its own storage. Keeping your phone locked with a PIN, pattern or fingerprint protects it further.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features such as ads, a premium subscription or cloud sync, we will update this policy before that version is released. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
