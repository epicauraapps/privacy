Privacy Policy

Tools Pocket – Daily Utilities · EpicAura Apps

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Tools Pocket – Daily Utilities (package com.epicauraapps.toolpocket), made by EpicAura Apps ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

In short: all tools work on your phone. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, photos, scanned codes, passwords or text. We use Google Firebase to understand app usage, fix crashes and change app settings remotely.

1. Information that stays on your phone

The following is processed only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Camera (Barcode Scanner, QR Scanner, Magnifier, Flashlight): the camera image is used live to scan codes, zoom or turn on the light. No photo or video is saved or uploaded.
  • Images you pick in the Magnifier: opened only to view them on your phone.
  • Scan results, text, numbers and calculations (calculators, converters, Text Case Converter): stay on your phone. They are shared only if you tap Copy or Share yourself.
  • Passwords made by the Password Generator: created on your phone and never stored or sent. When copied, they are marked as sensitive so Android hides them in the clipboard preview.
  • Settings such as your chosen theme (System, Light or Dark) are saved on your phone.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following services from Google. They may collect information automatically:

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Firebase Analytics App usage events (for example app opens and screens viewed), app instance ID, Android advertising ID, device model, Android version, app version, language, and approximate country (from IP address). To understand which tools people use and improve the app.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports: stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, free memory and disk, and a random installation ID. To find and fix crashes.
Firebase Remote Config A random installation ID, app version and country. To change app settings without a new update.

This information does not identify you by name. Please read Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. How we use information

  • To keep the app working and fix crashes.
  • To understand which tools are popular and plan new features.
  • To change app settings remotely.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to identify you personally.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: for scanning, the magnifier and the flashlight. Asked only when you open these tools. You can deny it and still use all other tools.
  • Internet and network state: used only by Firebase to send usage and crash data. All tools work without internet.
  • Advertising ID: used by Firebase Analytics for usage statistics.

5. Data retention

Firebase Analytics data is kept for a limited time (up to 14 months). Crashlytics crash reports are kept for about 90 days. After that, Google deletes them automatically.

6. Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can turn off the camera permission in Settings → Apps → Tools Pocket → Permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your phone.
  • To ask for deletion of data linked to your installation, email us (see below).

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method is 100% secure, but we use trusted services and collect as little as possible.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
